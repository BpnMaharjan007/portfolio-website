Grid-forming inverters · islanded microgrids

Making nonlinear inverter dynamics linear enough to control.

I'm a graduate electrical engineering researcher. My thesis lifts the dynamics of droop-controlled grid-forming inverters into a Koopman observable space, where a linear predictor is accurate enough to drive a constrained MPC — and then checks that claim against a reinforcement learning baseline on the same testbed.

Research

Four threads, one testbed

Everything below runs against the same islanded AC microgrid model, so the methods can actually be compared rather than just described.

  • Thesis core

    Koopman–EDMDc model predictive control

    A mode-aware lifting pipeline for droop-controlled grid-forming inverters: sine and cosine embeddings of the power angle in grid-tied mode, angle observables dropped when islanded. Feeding a velocity-form MPC with a Hildreth QP gives offset-free tracking under input and rate constraints.

    MATLAB · Simulink · EDMDc · constrained QP

  • Baseline

    Multi-agent RL for secondary control

    A MADDPG implementation of distributed secondary control, replacing a centralised DDPG critic with centralised training and decentralised execution. The interesting case isn't nominal performance — it's what happens when the communication link between agents drops.

    Python · PyTorch · Gymnasium · pandapower

  • Comparison track

    Physics-informed neural control

    Two strands: a PINC-style distributed secondary voltage controller for a low-voltage inverter microgrid, and a physics-informed predictor driving MPC on a DC–DC buck converter feeding a constant power load.

    PINN · PINC · averaged CCM model

  • Side build

    CircuitLab, a browser circuit simulator

    Modified nodal analysis with companion models, running entirely client-side, with a phosphor-style oscilloscope readout. Verified against buck, boost, RC, RLC, and half-wave rectifier circuits.

    React · MNA · numerical integration
